Overview

A-Cursors respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the A-Cursors website.

Information We Collect

A-Cursors is primarily designed for browsing and discovering cursor resources. We do not intentionally require users to provide personal information simply to browse the website.

Searches and Preferences

Certain website preferences, such as the selected light or dark theme, may be stored locally in your browser. This information is used to provide a more convenient browsing experience.

Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember preferences. These technologies generally remain on your device and are not used by A-Cursors as a personal identity system.

Third-Party Services

A-Cursors may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertisements, content delivery, or external downloads. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

External Links

A-Cursors may contain links to external websites or download locations. Once you leave A-Cursors, the privacy practices of the external website apply.

Children's Privacy

A-Cursors does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
